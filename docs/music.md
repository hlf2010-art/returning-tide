# Music in the world

There is no score laid over the game. The music you hear comes from a source in the world: Manner's playlist behind its window on Hankou Road, the doors of two stores on Nanjing Road East while they are open, and a DiDi driver's radio. Placement and loudness: `src/engine/speakers.js`. Playback: `src/world/music.js`. Off switch and credits: Settings, under "Music in shops and cars".

## The tracks: public domain, no licence and no royalties

All fourteen are modern instrumentals that their composers dedicated to the public domain under **CC0 1.0** (https://creativecommons.org/publicdomain/zero/1.0/) on OpenGameArt. CC0 sets no conditions: the game may use, ship and adapt them, no attribution is required and no royalty is owed. They are credited anyway, as a courtesy.

Checked on each track's own page on 18 November 2026:
- CC0 is the only licence listed.
- The description gives no sign of samples, covers or remixes of someone else's work.
- The music is an original composition. *Giant Steps but smooth* was rejected: it re-arranges John Coltrane's "Giant Steps", a composition still under copyright, which an uploader's CC0 cannot clear.

Processing: levelled to -18 LUFS (true peak -1.5 dB), mixed to mono, 44.1 kHz, 80 kbps MP3 (25 MB in all). Nothing else was changed.

## Local artists

The owner wants local, contemporary Shanghai and Chinese music. Almost none of it is under a licence that allows a game to ship it, so local artists are being asked directly (the request letter is in Notion). A track joins a playlist with a line in `TRACKS` and `PLAYLISTS` and its file in `public/music/`, with the artist's written permission kept on file.

## The sightseeing train (南京路步行街观光车, 铛铛车)

- **Look** (a photo of November 2025, from behind): white bodies and roof posts, a green arched canopy with a scalloped valance (carrying sponsors' ads in reality), a green skirt, a fleet number on the back (11), forward-facing benches. The tractor wasn't visible: the game's is a tram-fronted unit in the same colours. Earlier trains (2007, 2008) were 1930s-tram replicas.
- **Route:** Henan Road to Xizang Road, not on the 2020 extension to the Bund. The 1999 street has a 4.2 m red granite strip (金带) 1.3 m north of the centre line and a 7 m sightseeing lane south of it: the game has both. Where it turns round isn't documented; the game turns at each end. Stops: the two ends and Century Square (tickets were sold at the west end and in the middle); ticket kiosks there.
- **Running:** battery-electric, rubber tyres, about 5–8 km/h (the game: 6), about 10 minutes end to end; 08:30–22:00 (sources disagree: 08:00–22:30, 09:30–22:00); the bell gives it its name. Fare ¥5 (or ¥10; not shown in the game).
- **Unknown:** the number of cars, their size and seats, how often they run (fleet number 11 suggests several trains). The game runs two trains of a tractor and three trailers.

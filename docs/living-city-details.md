# Living city: the detail bible

*Started 25 November 2026. The small things that make the northern Bund read as Shanghai, just the way
it is. Companion to `docs/living-city.md` (targets) and `docs/living-city-architecture.md` (the
systems these details hang on).*

## How to use this list

- **One row per detail**, with where and when it happens, how often, what it rests on, and a rough
  cost. A detail is "done" only when it has been checked against a reference.
- **Eye level first.** Every detail pass starts within 3 m of the player: hands, faces, counters,
  the pavement. Nobody inspects a roof.
- **Reference beats memory.** "Local knowledge" rows are written from knowledge of the city and must
  be confirmed in footage (ideally the owner's own photos and recordings) before or after they are
  built. A wrong one is a table edit, not a rewrite: keep these as data where possible.
- **A detail pass** takes 8-10 rows, builds the cheap ones, and checks them in screenshots or
  recordings against the reference.

What good detail does, and the test for every row:
1. **It reacts** to the player, the weather, the hour or other people. Static props don't immerse.
2. **It's culturally specific.** A local should smile in recognition.
3. **It leaves traces**: the world has a past and a next hour (litter after a crowd, a sweeper the
   next morning).
4. **It rewards a closer look**: it still holds up when you stop and stare.

Cost: **S** a sound, a prop or a small behaviour (hours); **M** a small system (a day or two);
**L** depends on a phase of the architecture plan. Status: `idea`, `built`, `checked` (against a
reference).

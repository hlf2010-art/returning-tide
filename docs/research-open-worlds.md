# Research: what makes AAA open worlds feel alive (GTA 6, RDR2, Cyberpunk 2077, inZOI)

*25 November 2026, background research (web sources as linked). GTA 6 launched on 19 November 2026; its notes
rely on reviews and reported interviews. Corrected for our game: the protagonist is Ji Bai (a man), and the
map is the northern Bund, Nanjing Road East and Line 2 (the report's lane ideas are kept as ideas only).*

## GTA 6
- A dedicated NPC team; 600,000+ NPC animations; personality traits drive behaviour ([GameRant/Edge](https://gamerant.com/gta6-npc-animations-behaviors-traits/)); ~1,000 everyday animation sets; passers-by get the leads' hair and cloth tech ([Creative Bloq](https://www.creativebloq.com/news/gta-6s-npcs-are-built-to-the-same-visual-standard-as-jason-and-lucia)).
- 3-4x the dialogue per NPC; groups (joggers, couples) keep talking after you pass ([Vice](https://www.vice.com/en/article/gta-6-review/)); timetabled buses and trains; your actions show up in social posts, news and a radio recap ([Kotaku](https://kotaku.com/grand-theft-auto-6-the-kotaku-review-2000757842)); local rain cells and lingering puddles ([Dexerto](https://www.dexerto.com/gta/gtas-6s-weather-system-revealed-with-hurricanes-tropical-storms-dynamic-wind-3413591/)); police remember your car and clothes.
- Loved: 100+ distinct NPCs in one tram station; small physical details; bikers robbing a jeweller while you watch; the partner reacting without input. Criticised: 30 fps dips in crowds; shops open at 3 a.m.; mixed-up prompts.

## Red Dead Redemption 2
- Full-day routines ([PC Gamer](https://pcgamer.com/red-dead-redemption-2-npcs-lead-interesting-lives)); witnesses report crimes ([GameSpot](https://www.gamespot.com/articles/red-dead-redemption-2s-world-is-full-of-snitches/1100-6462203/)); honour changes greetings and prices; ~500k lines; layered live music.
- Remembered: the snakebite stranger who later buys you a gun; NPCs remembering a theft; growing beards, mud. Criticised: animations that take control away ("animation priority").

## Cyberpunk 2077 (first person, like us)
- Launch: pedestrians cowered and vanished, police spawned in view; fixed over patches (1.5 crowd reactions, 2.0 traffic and police, 2.1 a rideable metro, sitting at bars, working kiosks) ([2.1 notes](https://www.shacknews.com/article/137986/cyberpunk-2077-update-2-1-patch-notes)).
- First person even in cutscenes; you keep moving while people talk; "hidden choices" acted mid-scene ([GDC 2024 via Game Developer](https://gamedeveloper.com/design/hidden-choices-small-ripples-how-cdpr-merged-cinematics-and-interactivity-in-cyberpunk-2077)); your hands in every action; quick vaults and slides; personality through quips; street chatter subtitled as a translation implant.

## inZOI
- Up to 300 Zois per city spreading rumours, trends and flu ([GamesRadar](https://gamesradar.com/games/simulation/the-sims-rival-inzoi-has-300-people-hanging-out-in-each-of-its-in-game-cities-and-they-all-interact-with-each-other-in-real-time)); "Smart Zoi" runs a small local language model (RTX only); readable thoughts; plans rewritten nightly.
- Criticised: beautiful but "almost empty"; strange AI choices ([NoobFeed](https://noobfeed.com/reviews/inzoi-review)).

## For us: the top ten, by "alive" per unit of effort
1. **Layered sound by place and hour.** (Done in part: the recorded beds, payment speakers, the clock.) Next: shop loudspeakers, the tram bell.
2. **One clock runs the district.** Breakfast, commuter waves, dusk on the promenade, shutters at night; no barber at 3 a.m.
3. **Looping scenes in fixed places** (Cyberpunk's fixed groups, RDR2 routines): wedding shoots on the promenade, a guard dozing in his booth, the clock swapping them in and out.
4. **Crowds that never pop.** (Phase 1 done: tiers, spawning out of sight.) Next: waves out of the metro on each train.
5. **Glances and remarks that depend on Ji Bai's state**: soaked, carrying shopping, out at 2 a.m., dressed "American". A few hundred situation-matched lines beat thousands of generic ones.
6. **A small cast that remembers him, and gossip**: 15-20 regulars with a memory of him; news spreading over a day.
7. **The phone as the world's memory** (GTA 6): family chat forwarding articles, friends reacting to his Bund photo, a payment app refusing his US card. (Partly there: the viral video, the group chat.)
8. **Weather that leaves traces**: puddles that stay, umbrellas, shelter under arcades, riders in ponchos.
9. **Visible hands and an inner voice** (Cyberpunk): his hands in every interaction; a bilingual inner monologue ("wait - 怎么扫?"); short, interruptible actions.
10. **A real-time metro ride.** (Done: Line 2.)

## What not to copy
Rockstar's scale; a local language model driving NPCs (Safari can't run it; inZOI's output was often incoherent; use AI to write content ahead of time instead); a police and wanted system; ray tracing and simulated clouds; every door enterable (empty interiors feel dead); long animations that take control away.

## Three signature moments
1. **Lights out at ten.** The skyline and façades go dark; the railing sighs, tripods fold, the river becomes audible. (Built in part: the sigh, the railing emptying.)
2. **Umbrellas bloom.** A sudden downpour on Nanjing Road: hundreds of umbrellas open in seconds, a vendor appears selling them for ¥10, people crowd under awnings, neon smears on wet stone; Dad texts "带伞了吗?".
3. **The city knows he's back.** An older shopkeeper squints: "美国回来啦?"; Shanghainese subtitles half-garbled at first, filling in as his ear comes back; by evening the group chat has opinions.
